## 🚀 Projects

- **[Factorbox](https://factorbox.ir)**: free online invoice maker. Create sales invoices, pro-formas and receipts right on the page, then print or export to PDF and Excel. No sign-up, data stays on your device, and an MCP server lets AI assistants draft invoices.
- **[Locio](https://locio.ir)**: store locator and location pages for businesses. Put one shop or hundreds of branches on a live, shareable map with addresses, opening hours and contact info.
- **[amirsalehi.ir](https://amirsalehi.ir)**: my personal website, with my blog on JavaScript, React and web development, plus my resume and projects.
